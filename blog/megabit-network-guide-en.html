What can Wi-Fi analysis tools tell you?

Network tools provide useful connection indicators, but no single number tells the whole story or proves a security problem on its own.

When using Wi-Fi at home or work, you may want to understand signal strength, devices appearing on the network, and connection speed. These indicators can help, as long as you keep their limitations in mind.

Signal strength: where is reception better?

A signal-analysis tool shows how strongly your phone receives a Wi-Fi network. It can help compare locations near a router, but signal is not the only factor in connection quality. Network congestion, router capabilities, and your internet provider can also matter.

Connected devices: a list to review, not a final verdict

A network scan may show active devices reachable on the local network. Device names and details are not always clear, and a scan is not a substitute for router settings or good security practices. If a device looks unfamiliar, check your router’s administration panel and the devices you own before drawing conclusions.

Speed tests: readings depend on conditions

A speed result depends on the test time, Wi-Fi reception at the phone, other devices using the connection, and the test method. Repeat tests under different conditions and compare them rather than relying on one reading.

How does MegaBit help?

Important: checking connected devices or measuring speed alone does not prove that a network is secure or that an internet provider is delivering its full service. These are helpful indicators, not a complete security audit.
