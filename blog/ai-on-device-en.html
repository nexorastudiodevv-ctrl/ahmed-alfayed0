AI on-device or in the cloud?

Where an AI model should run depends on the task, data sensitivity, and device capabilities—not on a single option that fits every situation.

An AI feature in an app may process data on the phone, on cloud servers, or through a combination of both. Understanding the trade-offs helps developers and users choose an appropriate experience.

What does on-device processing mean?

With local processing, the phone uses its own resources to perform a task. Once a model and required data are available on the device, the task may rely less on an internet connection and may not need to send its inputs to an external server. This does not automatically mean the entire app works offline or sends no data for other purposes; always review the app’s privacy policy and settings.

Phones have limited resources compared with large servers. Processing speed, model size, and task complexity can therefore depend on a device’s memory, capabilities, and power use.

What about cloud processing?

With a cloud approach, an app sends a request to an online service, and remote servers handle the processing. This can make larger models or capabilities available, but requires a suitable connection and may involve transferring data to a service provider, depending on the product and its policies.

Response time depends on both the network and the service. Developers should explain what data is sent, how it is used, and how long it is retained.

How should you choose?

  • For sensitive data: consider local processing or minimizing transferred data, and verify how the app actually behaves.
  • For demanding or frequently changing tasks: a cloud service may be practical, provided its provider and connectivity requirements are clear.
  • For balance: a hybrid design can handle simple tasks locally and use the cloud when needed, with clear user consent.
The takeaway: calling a feature “on-device AI” alone does not establish its privacy or quality. Check the app’s actual behavior and privacy policy, and choose an approach that fits the task.
