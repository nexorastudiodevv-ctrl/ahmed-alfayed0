I’m Ahmed AlFayed, an Android and web developer from Egypt and the founder of Nexora Studio EG. I have one year of experience building apps from idea to release.

I build Android apps with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, using MVVM and Clean Architecture to keep code organized and maintainable. I’ve published more than three apps on Google Play and use Java when needed. For the web, I build fast, responsive sites with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
